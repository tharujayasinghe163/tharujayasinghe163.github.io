Mechanical Design Project

Coffee Bean Roaster

A manually operated coffee bean roaster developed from concept generation through SolidWorks modelling, assembly development and final engineering drawing.

SolidWorksMechanical DesignCAD AssemblyEngineering Drawing

Project Overview

The project focused on developing a practical coffee bean roasting mechanism. Several concepts were considered before selecting a manually operated design. The selected concept uses a hand crank to rotate a flywheel, providing the mechanical input for the roasting mechanism. The final design was developed as a SolidWorks assembly and documented using an engineering drawing.

Concept Development

Four different concepts were developed during the initial design stage. The concepts explored different methods of driving the flywheel and different arrangements of the roasting drum.

Selected Concept

Concept 01 — Manual Operation

A manually operated roaster in which a hand crank is used to rotate the flywheel and operate the roasting mechanism.

Concept 02 — Electric Motor

An alternative concept using an electric motor to rotate the flywheel.

Concept 03 — Vertical Drum

A concept in which the rotating roasting drum is arranged vertically.

Concept 04 — Spiral Form

A concept exploring a spiral-shaped roasting arrangement.

Selected Concept

Manual Coffee Bean Roaster

Concept 01 was selected as the final concept. The design is manually operated: the user turns a hand crank, which rotates the flywheel and drives the roasting mechanism.

This concept was then developed further into the final CAD assembly.

CAD Development & Final Assembly

The selected concept was developed into a complete SolidWorks assembly. The final model was examined from multiple views to communicate the overall arrangement and mechanical components of the roaster.
